### Definitions

# Offtune
- A bar that fills, unique to each enemy (max value uncertain)
- When the bar is full, the enemy enters 'mistune' state
- Might scale with the damage dealt and action offtune multiplier, or a flat value based on action
- Might have 400.000 max capacity

# Mistune
- This state does nothing by itself, but when exists allow characters to cast tunebreak skills

# Tunebreak Skills
- Stops time while casting it, so technically cast time of tunebreak skills is 0
- Damage taken is based on the type of enemy class (calmity, boss, mob, etc)
- Set multiplier value of 1600 %
- Affected by enemy resistances - seemingly deals physical damage
- Not affected by damage bonuses or amplification or crit
- Affected by the tunebreak stat (1 % tunebreak = 1 % more damage)
- Affected by damage taken affects: "X take Y & more damage, like Cartethyia S6"
- Enemies offtune bar will go back to 0 and there will be a cooldown where it's locked before you can start filling it up again

# Tune Rupture Shifting (debuff)
- Does nothing on its own
- When an enemy is hit by a tunebreak skill, 'Tune Rupture - Shifting' turns into 'Tune Rupture - Interfered'
- Tune Rupture - Interfered: When performing Tunebreak Skills deals an extra instance of damage  unique to the character.
- Lynae has a multiplier of 1880.75 % (now scales with spectro instead of physical)

# Tune Strain Shifting (debuff)
- Does nothing on its own
- When an enemy is hit by a tunebreak skill
- When an enemy is hit by a tunebreak skill, 'Tune Strain - Shifting' turns into 'Tune Strain - Interfered'
- When a character perform Tunebreak Response, it deals additional damage
- The scaling is 0.12 % for every stack of tunebreak boost you have (for example Lynae has 10 + 40 % -> 6 % extra damage)
- Also multiplies the damage dealt by the number of tune strain stacks on the target (Lynae increase stack cap by 1, i.e. 6 % x 2 stacks = 12 % extra damage)

# Tunebreak Response
- A character performs tunebreak, allowing characters to respond to it
